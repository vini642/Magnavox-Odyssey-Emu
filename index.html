        Important: pins are not commands. A line such as
        6-8-14 means those contacts are physically
        joined into one electrical net. There is no left-to-right direction.

Homebrew Workshop

    make your own Odyssey game

    

        1. Pick what the console should physically do.

        Start from a stock card that behaves similarly, or edit the copper connections manually.
        The cartridge is just groups of pins that are electrically joined.
    


    

        2. Write the “ROM”.

        Each line is one electrical net:
        
2-4
6-8-14-16-20-22
30-34

        A line like 6-8-14 means all three pins share one net.
    


    

        3. Add an overlay.

        Make a 4:3 PNG in any editor, upload it above, then tune the opacity until the white spots
        line up with your artwork.
    


    

        4. Invent the rules.

        The Odyssey hardware only knows spots, coincidence, flip-flops, and Crowbar-style suppression.
        Scoring, objectives, dice, cards, tokens, money, etc. can all live outside the machine.
    


    

        5. Use Advanced diagnostics when you get stuck.

        Open the optional diagnostics section to inspect active modules, live pins,
        coincidence signals, Crowbar state, and the event log.
